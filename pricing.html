What you get for about $20 a month

  • ChatGPT Plus ($20): expanded memory and context, scheduled and event-triggered tasks, the option to buy extra credits.
  • Google AI Pro ($19.99): the Pro model, Daily Brief, Gemini in Gmail and Docs, Chrome auto browse, 5 TB of storage.
  • Claude Pro ($20): handed-off and scheduled tasks, Research, Projects, Claude in Chrome and Microsoft 365.
  • Microsoft 365 Premium ($19.99): Office apps with extensive Copilot usage, limited agentic AI, 2 TB of shared storage.
  • Perplexity Pro ($20): advanced models, Pro Search, file analysis and a browser-agent allowance in Comet.
  • Alexa+ without Prime ($19.99): the full Alexa+ experience across devices for the household.

Pricing tips

  • If you already pay for Prime, Microsoft 365 or Google One, check what AI features you already have before buying another plan.
  • Credit-based tools (Lindy, Motion) cost more as your workload grows, so estimate your usage before committing.
  • Prices change often. Always confirm on the official pricing page linked above.
